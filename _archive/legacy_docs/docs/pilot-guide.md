# HungerRush Cadence — Pilot Guide

Welcome to Cadence, the HungerRush 1:1 briefing tool. It turns your team's weekly
metrics into talking points for better 1:1 conversations — focused on growth and
momentum, never on judgment.

## Getting started

### Logging in

1. Go to **https://hungerrush-scorecard.vercel.app**
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**
3. Use your regular HungerRush Microsoft 365 credentials
4. You'll land on **Your team** — a roster of your people with a 1:1 briefing below

No separate account or password needed — it uses the same login as Outlook and Teams.

### First-time walkthrough

On your first visit, a short tour explains the key features. Close it anytime with
the X, the Esc key, or by clicking outside it — it won't come back once dismissed.

## Your team (the roster)

The strip across the top shows your people. Each chip carries:

- A **tone dot** — teal for a strong stretch, terracotta when something's worth
  discussing, gray for steady or still-new
- A short summary — "on track", "one to discuss", "focus this week", "ramping",
  or "no data yet"
- When their last 1:1 was

Use the search box to find someone, and the **With data / All** pills to include
people whose metrics haven't started syncing. The roster is alphabetical — Cadence
never ranks people.

## The 1:1 briefing

Selecting a person opens their briefing — designed to be the tab you have open
during the conversation:

- **Talking points**, ordered discuss → celebrate. The top one is flagged
  **start here**, and the lead points carry a suggested opening question. A steady
  week says so; a new hire's briefing reminds you to keep it about onboarding
  (trends unlock at week 4).
- **Action items** carried over from your recent sessions (last 12 weeks), so
  commitments follow into the next 1:1. Tick them off as they land.
- **Notes** — your session history, grouped by week. Add a note and action items
  for today's session; everything persists and is visible only to you.

## The evidence panel

Metrics sit beside the briefing as supporting evidence, grouped by source
(Zendesk, Assembled). Each row shows:

- The metric name with its tone dot and trend
- An honest sparkline over the last 8 weeks — fixed scale, so small wiggles look
  small; occupancy shades its healthy range; missing weeks stay visible gaps
- **Both data windows, labeled**: this week so far (refreshed every 4 hours) and
  last week (completed — frozen at Sunday's snapshot)
- A coaching prompt — a conversation starter for that metric

**How trends are read:** the latest value is compared with the average of the
previous few weeks (up to 4). Within ±6% counts as steady. Count metrics (like
ticket volume) measure their trend through the last *completed* week, so a quiet
Monday morning never reads as a collapse. Fewer than 4 weeks of history shows as
"new".

Each source group shows how many weeks of history it has and when it last synced.
If a person's data hasn't synced recently, an amber banner says so and the panel
shows the last synced values — amber always means "data freshness", never a
judgment about the person.

Some rows may read "No data yet" or "Not configured" — normal; that data source
isn't active for that person yet.

## Sharing a scorecard

Share a read-only snapshot with the person it's about:

1. Open their briefing and click **Share**
2. A link is copied — valid for 72 hours, reusable until it expires
3. Send it to the employee — they can view their own metrics without logging in

The shared page frames the numbers as a conversation starter, shows a synced
timestamp on every metric, and every open of the link is logged.

## PDF export

**Export PDF** downloads a briefing snapshot: every metric with both weekly
windows and its trend word, coaching prompts, and a watermark recording who
exported it and when. Exports are logged for the admin.

## Team rollup (senior managers and executives)

If you manage managers, **Team rollup** appears in the navigation. Each manager
card shows trend counts across their team — how many metrics are improving, how
many are worth discussing — plus per-metric chips in the same words. Click a card
to open that manager's full team. It points attention; it never scores or ranks
anyone.

## On your phone

Below tablet width the navigation folds into the menu button (top-left), and the
roster scrolls sideways. Cadence also installs as an app:

- **Desktop (Chrome):** click the install icon in the address bar
- **Android:** "Add to Home Screen" when prompted
- **iOS (Safari):** share icon → "Add to Home Screen"

## Offline access

If you lose internet briefly, a banner appears and Cadence shows cached data from
your last visit. It refreshes automatically when you reconnect.
