# Manager Scorecard — Pilot Guide

Welcome to the HungerRush Manager Scorecard. This tool gives you a clear view of your team's weekly metrics so you can have better 1:1 conversations focused on growth and momentum.

## Getting started

### Logging in

1. Go to **https://hungerrush-scorecard.vercel.app**
2. Click **Sign in with Microsoft**
3. Use your regular HungerRush Microsoft 365 credentials
4. You'll land on your dashboard showing your direct reports

No separate account or password needed — it uses the same login as Outlook and Teams.

### First-time walkthrough

On your first login, a short 4-step tour will explain the key features. You can re-open this tour anytime by clicking the **?** icon in the top navigation bar.

## Using the dashboard

Your dashboard shows all your direct reports. Each employee card shows whether metric data is available.

- **Filter by metrics** — use the dropdown to show only employees with metric data, or view all employees
- **Search** — type a name to quickly find someone
- **Click any employee** to open their scorecard

## Reading a scorecard

Each scorecard shows two data windows:

- **This week so far** — the latest synced data, refreshed every 4 hours
- **Last week (completed)** — a frozen snapshot from the end of last week, ideal for 1:1 discussions

### Metric tiles

Each metric tile shows:

- The metric name and current value
- A direction indicator showing whether the metric is improving
- A 4-week sparkline showing the recent trend
- A coaching prompt — a suggested conversation starter for your 1:1

Coaching prompts are designed to keep conversations growth-oriented. They focus on what's improving and where there's opportunity, never on blame.

### Metrics you'll see

- **Ticket volume** — number of tickets handled
- **First reply time** — how quickly the first response goes out
- **Resolution rate** — percentage of tickets fully resolved
- **CSAT score** — customer satisfaction from post-interaction surveys

Some metrics may show "No data" or "Not configured" — this is normal and means the data source isn't active for that employee yet.

## 1:1 notes

Click **New Session** on any employee's scorecard to start a 1:1 note.

- Add free-text notes about your conversation
- Create **action items** with checkboxes you can mark complete over time
- Notes are saved automatically and visible only to you
- Previous sessions appear below so you can track progress across weeks

## Sharing a scorecard

You can share a read-only view of an employee's scorecard:

1. Open the employee's scorecard
2. Click **Share**
3. A link is generated that's valid for 72 hours
4. Send the link to the employee — they can view their own metrics without logging in
5. The link works multiple times until it expires

Shared views are watermarked and logged for security.

## PDF export

Click **Export PDF** on any scorecard to download a PDF snapshot. The PDF includes:

- All current metric values and trends
- Coaching prompts
- A watermark showing when and by whom it was generated

## Senior manager rollup

If you manage other managers, you'll see a **Rollup** view in the navigation. This shows aggregate trend direction across your managers' teams — for example, "6 of 8 metrics are improving this month." It highlights where to focus attention without ranking individuals.

## Installing as an app (optional)

The scorecard works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — you can install it on your phone or desktop for quick access:

- **Desktop (Chrome):** Click the install icon in the address bar, or look for "Install app" in the browser menu
- **Mobile (Android):** Tap "Add to Home Screen" when prompted
- **Mobile (iOS):** In Safari, tap the share icon, then "Add to Home Screen"

Once installed, it opens like a regular app with its own icon.

## Offline access

If you lose internet briefly, the app will show cached data from your last visit. A banner will appear letting you know you're offline. Data refreshes automatically when you reconnect.
